# HCLSoftware

> HCLSoftware: Orchestrating eXperience, Data and Operations at Scale

HCLSoftware is the software business division of HCLTech. It develops, markets, sells, and supports enterprise software across six primary areas: Business & Industry Applications, AI & Intelligent Operations, Total Experience, Data & Analytics, Cybersecurity, and Sovereign Collaboration. A separate Specialized Software portfolio addresses focused manufacturing, mainframe, engineering, and secure software delivery use cases.

Core value propositions center on combining intelligent experiences, trusted data, automation, security, and sovereign collaboration. Common use cases include marketing automation, digital commerce, customer data management, endpoint management, application security, workload orchestration, analytics, low-code development, secure collaboration, DevSecOps, CAD/CAM, and mainframe optimization.
